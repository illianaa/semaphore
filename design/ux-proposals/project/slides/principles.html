Principles

Five principles shape all three proposals

1

One place to start, one message to send.

Starting a group chat should feel like starting any chat.

2

The plumbing stays out of sight.

No setup commands for you to manage. Claude and Astra handle the protocol.

3

You can always tell whose turn it is.

Status claims only what Semaphore can prove: waiting, received, replied.

4

Speak anytime, and pick who replies.

Even while an AI has the stick. Choosing who replies first stays everywhere.

5

Typing feels like the apps you know.

Enter sends, the box grows as you write, and the text is easy to read.
